How to Bid

Plain steps for each marketplace we use.

Do I bid on this website?

No. Our site is the shop window. Each lot has a Bid button that opens the marketplace where the auction runs. You bid, pay and get buyer protection there.

How do I bid on eBay?
  1. Click “Bid on eBay” on any lot.
  2. Sign in to eBay or create a free account.
  3. Enter your maximum bid. eBay bids for you up to that amount.
  4. If you win, pay through eBay. We ship fast and insured.

Core channel (Phase 1). Raw cards worth a single listing, plus lots.

See the eBay placeholder page ›

How do I bid on Fanatics Collect?
  1. Click “Bid on Fanatics Collect”.
  2. Sign in or create a Fanatics Collect account.
  3. Place your bid before the auction closes.
  4. Fanatics Collect invoices the winner and ships the graded card.

Graded, higher-value cards (Phase 2). We consign; they run the auction.

See the Fanatics Collect placeholder page ›

How do I bid on MySlabs?
  1. Click “Buy on MySlabs”.
  2. Sign in or create a MySlabs account.
  3. Buy at the listed price or make an offer if allowed.
  4. Pay on MySlabs; the card ships to you.

Graded cards we self-list (Phase 2).

See the MySlabs placeholder page ›

How do I bid on COMC?
  1. Click “Buy on COMC”.
  2. Browse the cards COMC scanned from Ron’s box.
  3. Add the ones you want to your COMC cart.
  4. Pay on COMC; they ship from their warehouse.

Bulk commons (Phase 2). We send boxes; COMC scans and lists each card.

See the COMC placeholder page ›

How do I bid on GreatCollections?
  1. Click “Bid on GreatCollections”.
  2. Register for a GreatCollections bidder account.
  3. Place a bid before the lot closes.
  4. Pay GreatCollections; they ship your coin insured.

Coins (Phase 2).

See the GreatCollections placeholder page ›

How do I bid on Hake's?
  1. Click “Bid on Hake's”.
  2. Register as a bidder with Hake's.
  3. Bid online during the auction.
  4. Hake's invoices the winner and ships.

Collectible toys (Phase 2).

See the Hake's placeholder page ›

How do I bid on LiveAuctioneers?
  1. Click “Bid on LiveAuctioneers” (for Gold Coast catalog sales, later).
  2. Register for the sale on LiveAuctioneers.
  3. Leave an absentee bid or bid live on sale day.
  4. Pay through LiveAuctioneers after the sale.

Gold Coast branded catalog sales (Phase 3, later).

See the LiveAuctioneers placeholder page ›

How do I bid on HiBid?
  1. Click “Bid on HiBid” (for Gold Coast catalog sales, later).
  2. Register for the sale on HiBid.
  3. Bid online until lots close.
  4. Pay through HiBid after the sale.

Alternative for Gold Coast branded catalog sales (Phase 3, later).

See the HiBid placeholder page ›

How do I bid on Whatnot?
  1. Click “Join the show on Whatnot” (later).
  2. Download Whatnot or open it on the web and sign in.
  3. Bid in real time as Ron shows each item on camera.
  4. Whatnot handles payment and the shipping label.

Live bulk sales (Phase 3, later).

Is my payment safe?

Yes. You pay the marketplace, not us, so their buyer protection applies.

Do you combine shipping?

On eBay we combine shipping for multiple wins from the same auction where we can. Details to be confirmed.

Can I see a card in person?

Contact us. Local pickup in Boca Raton may be possible for high-value lots (to be decided).
